## Play Console steps

1. Grow users → Store presence → Main store listing → paste copy from `04-store-listing-copy.md` → Save.
2. Policy status → open the impersonation issue → Submit an appeal.
3. Paste `03-play-console-appeal.md`.
4. Attach the two signed PDFs.
5. Submit once. Do not send a second appeal until Google replies.

## Typical outcome

Review is often 1–7 days. If rejected, the reply usually says the document was unsigned, not from the rights holder, or did not name this package. Fix that and appeal again with a new signed original. Do not argue by email.
