## Full description

Royal Kings Admin is the staff ERP mobile app published for Royal Kings Premier School LTD (Royal Kings School, Wangige, Kenya). It is for authorised school administrators and secretaries only.

WHAT YOU CAN DO
• Open the day-to-day operations dashboard and key school KPIs
• Manage students, admissions, and people records
• Track fees, payments, and finance summaries
• Handle HR and staff tools, including attendance geofencing where enabled
• Access operations, inventory, and school reports
• Receive official alerts with optional push notifications

SIGN IN WITH SCHOOL CREDENTIALS
• Sign in with your school-issued staff account
• Privacy and security: data is sent over HTTPS to the school’s ERP
• Biometric unlock is optional and stays on your device

WHO IT IS FOR
This app is for authorised Royal Kings School staff only. It is not for students, parents, or the general public. You need a school-issued staff account to sign in.
